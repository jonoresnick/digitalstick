      About Digital Stick

      Every player has a number. We put yours on your stick.

      Digital Stick started with a simple itch: sticks all look the same on the rack, and a strip of tape with your number in marker doesn’t last a week. We wanted something that looked like it came from the factory and still felt like yours.

      So we built a customizer. You choose two characters, a font, a fill colour and an outline. We print it on 1” vinyl and ship it in a pack, so you have one for every new twig this season.

      The fonts are named after the words you hear on the bench, and every colour comes straight from an NHL team palette, down to the hex code.

      Applying your sticker

      1. Clean the spot. Wipe the shaft with rubbing alcohol and let it dry.
      2. Peel and place. Line it up square with the shaft before you press down.
      3. Press it flat. Work from the centre out to push out any air.
      4. Let it set. Give it a few hours before your next skate.

        Questions

        How big are the stickers?

        Every sticker is 1” x 1”, which fits across the shaft of a senior or intermediate stick.

        Can I use one character?

        Yes. Enter a single number or letter and the design centres it for you.

        Can I use a colour that isn’t listed?

        Tap the plus button at the start of either colour row to pick any colour you like.

        Can I order more than one design?

        Yes. Add a design to your bag, then go back and build another. Every design ships together.
